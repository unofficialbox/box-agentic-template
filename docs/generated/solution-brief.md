# Commercial Loan Review: solution brief

## Business request

Help a loan officer review a borrower term sheet against the bank record and policy, propose the accepted bank terms, and check a draft commitment before signature preparation. Keep approval with the credit officer.

## Intended outcome

A source-cited loan review and a checked draft, with human credit approval retained.

## Authorities

- Governed content: Box
- Structured record: Example CRM
- Consequential decision: Credit officer

## Assumptions

- All records, policies, and documents in the included example are synthetic.
- Live adapters are not included.

## Not implemented

- Live Box or record-system adapters
- Document generation and signature preparation
- A borrower portal or hosted model orchestration
